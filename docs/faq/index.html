FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Short answers about how Baracut Reader works. For step-by-step help, see Support.

Which versions of Windows are supported?

Windows 10 (version 22H2) or Windows 11, on 64-bit Intel or AMD (x64) PCs. Other processor types aren't offered yet.

Do I need an internet connection?

Only to download the installer and updates. Opening, editing and saving PDFs works entirely offline. If the app can't reach the update service, it says so and keeps working normally.

How do I install it?

Download the installer from the download page and run it. Choose whether to add Baracut Reader to Open with for PDF files and whether you'd like a desktop shortcut, then choose Install. It installs for your Windows account in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Baracut Reader and doesn't need administrator permission. You'll find it in the Start menu.

How do updates work?

About once a day, Baracut Reader checks whether a newer version exists. If there is one, a small notice appears; nothing downloads or installs until you choose to. The download runs in the background while you keep working, and it is verified against signed release information before it can be installed. When you install, you're asked to save any unsaved changes, then the app closes, updates and opens again.

You can check at any time from More options → Check for updates, and turn automatic checks off in the Updates window.

How do I uninstall it?

Open Settings → Apps → Installed apps, find Baracut Reader and choose Uninstall. This removes the program, its shortcuts, its Open with entry, your saved signatures and any downloaded updates. Your PDFs are never touched. Your settings and diagnostic logs stay in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Baracut Reader; delete that folder if you want them gone too.

Are the signatures digital signatures?

No. Baracut Reader adds electronic signatures: a picture of your handwritten, typed or uploaded signature, placed on the page like signing a printout. They don't use certificates, and they don't prove who signed or that the document hasn't changed since; that is what certificate-based digital signatures do, which Baracut Reader doesn't offer yet. Whether an electronic signature is enough for a particular document depends on the document and on who asks for it.

Signatures you save stay on your PC, encrypted for your Windows account, and are never uploaded. You can delete them at any time in the Sign panel; uninstalling the app deletes them too.

Can I fill in PDF forms?

Yes. When a PDF has form fields, Baracut Reader finds them as it opens: type in text fields, check boxes, choose options and pick from lists, and move between fields with Tab. The form bar shows how many required fields are still empty. Your answers are saved in the PDF's own form, so other PDF apps show them and the form can still be filled later. Save flattened copy makes a copy where the answers become part of the pages.

Scanned or flat forms have no fields. For those, the Fill & Sign tool adds text, checkmarks, X marks, the date, your initials and your signature wherever you click.

Forms are filled without running their scripts, so calculated fields aren't updated. Forms made with XFA, an older Adobe form technology, can't be filled yet.

Can I search inside a PDF?

Yes. Press Ctrl+F and type. Baracut Reader searches every page as you type, highlights the matches and takes you to the nearest one; Enter or F3 goes to the next. The results list shows every match with the words around it. Capital letters and accents don't have to match, and you can ask for the exact case or whole words only. Text you added and answers in form fields are found too.

Search finds the text a PDF stores. A scanned page is a picture, so it has no text to find until text recognition (OCR) exists in the app; the search tells you when pages have no text. Searching happens on your PC: nothing you look for is saved or sent anywhere.

Where are my documents stored?

Wherever you keep them. Baracut Reader opens files from, and saves them to, the places you choose. While a document is open, it keeps a temporary working copy in your Windows temporary folder, which is deleted when you close the document. For a password-protected PDF that copy stays encrypted: the unlocked content is kept only in memory.

Does Baracut Reader upload my PDFs anywhere?

No. Your documents are opened, edited and saved on your computer. There is no account, no cloud storage and no analytics. The only internet connections the app makes are the update check and update downloads. Like any web request they reach the server with your computer's IP address, and they include the app's version number, but nothing about your documents. The privacy page has the details.

Does removing text with Edit text delete it from the file?

Not securely. Other PDF readers show the edited pages, but Baracut Reader keeps a copy of the original content inside the saved PDF so your changes stay editable when you reopen it. It is not a redaction tool: don't use it to hide confidential information.

Can I edit scanned PDFs?

You can highlight and add your own text on scanned pages. Changing the text of a scan needs text recognition (OCR), which isn't included yet.

Can it open password-protected PDFs?

Yes, when you have the password. Baracut Reader asks for it when you open the PDF; a wrong password is simply asked for again. You can then read, edit and save the PDF like any other, and saving keeps its password and restrictions.

To add a password, choose More options → Protect with password; to change or remove one, More options → Password protection. Protection uses 256-bit AES, the method of the current PDF standard, which current PDF readers and web browsers open. A PDF can also restrict printing, copying and changes with a separate permissions password; PDF readers are expected to honor those restrictions, but not every app does.

The password is used only on your PC, for that file. It is never saved, never written to logs and never sent anywhere.

I forgot a PDF's password. Can Baracut Reader recover it?

No. Baracut Reader opens a protected PDF only with its password, and it never guesses, recovers or gets around one. Ask whoever protected the PDF for the password, or for a copy without protection. Keep passwords you set somewhere safe, such as a password manager: without it, a protected PDF can't be opened.

How do I remove the password from a PDF?

Open the PDF with its password, choose More options → Password protection, then Remove protection, and save (or use Save as to keep the protected original). To keep the protected PDF and get a copy without a password, choose Save unprotected copy instead. If the PDF has a separate permissions password, Baracut Reader opens it for reading and asks for that password before its protection can be changed or removed.

Are my PDFs uploaded when I combine or split them?

No. Combining, splitting and extracting happen entirely on your PC; nothing is sent anywhere. The files you start from are only read, never changed: the result is always a new PDF, saved where you choose.

Is the installer digitally signed?

Not yet. Until it is, Windows SmartScreen may say the app is unrecognized. If you downloaded the installer from this website, you can confirm it's the right file by comparing its SHA-256 checksum with the one on the download page, then choose More info → Run anyway.
