Privacy

Privacy

Baracut Reader is a desktop app that works with your files on your computer. It doesn't have accounts, it doesn't upload your documents, and it doesn't collect analytics. This page explains exactly what does and doesn't leave your computer.

Last updated 25 September 2026.

The short version

  • Your documents are opened, edited and saved on your computer. They are never sent anywhere.
  • The app has no analytics, no telemetry and no advertising.
  • The app connects to the internet only to check for updates and to download them. You can turn automatic checks off.
  • This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts or fonts.

The desktop app

Your documents. Baracut Reader reads PDFs from, and saves them to, the locations you choose. While a document is open, a temporary working copy is kept in your Windows temporary folder and deleted when the document closes.

Settings and logs. Your update preference and the time of the last update check are stored in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Baracut Reader\settings.json. Technical details about errors are written to a log in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Baracut Reader\Logs, which may include the names and locations of files you opened. Both stay on your computer.

No telemetry. The app doesn't record or send how you use it, what you open, or crash reports.

Update checks

About once a day, and whenever you choose Check for updates, the app downloads a small, digitally signed file that describes the newest version, from hbarakat313.github.io. If you choose to update, it downloads the installer from github.com.

These requests contain the app's name and version number (for example BaracutReader/1.5.0). Like any web request, they reach the server with your computer's IP address. They contain nothing about you, your files or how you use the app. The organizations that host these files may keep short-lived technical logs, such as IP addresses, under their own privacy policies.

To stop automatic checks, open More options → Check for updates and clear Check for updates automatically.

This website

The website is a set of static pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads no scripts, fonts or images from other companies, so there is no cookie banner. The company hosting the site may record technical data such as IP addresses to serve pages and protect against abuse, under its own privacy policy.

When you download the installer, the file comes from github.com, which may record the download in the same way.

What we don't do

We don't sell or share personal data, we don't build profiles, and we don't receive your documents.

Changes to this page

If a future version changes how data is handled, this page will be updated first and the change will be noted in the changelog.
