Changelog

What changed in each version

Installed copies update themselves, so the newest version is usually a click away. Download 1.6.0

  1. Version 1.6.0

    Current

    27 September 2026

    Open and edit password-protected PDFs when you have the password, and protect a PDF, change its password or remove it.

    New

    • Open password-protected PDFs: Baracut Reader asks for the password and opens the PDF once it's right. A wrong password is simply asked for again, and Cancel leaves the document you had open as it was.
    • Edit protected PDFs like any other: add text, highlights, drawings, images and signatures, and rearrange pages. Saving keeps the PDF's password and restrictions.
    • Protect a PDF with a password: choose More options, then Protect with password. It's saved with 256-bit AES encryption, the method of the current PDF standard.
    • Change a PDF's password, or remove its protection, when you opened it with its password. The change applies when you save; until then you can undo it from the same place.
    • Optionally restrict printing, copying and changes with a separate permissions password. PDF readers are expected to honor these restrictions, but not every app does.
    • See how a PDF is protected: its encryption, how you opened it and what its permissions allow. A lock beside the document name shows that it's protected.
    • Save a password-protected copy of a PDF, or an unprotected copy of a protected one, without changing the document you have open.
    • Save as on a protected PDF asks whether the new file keeps its protection, gets a new password, or none.
    • A PDF opened with only its password to open, when its author also set a permissions password, opens for reading. Enter the permissions password to edit it.

    Improved

    • One password window for opening, combining and inserting pages, with a button to show what you typed and a Caps Lock hint.
    • A PDF added twice to Combine asks for its password only once. After combining protected PDFs, you're told the new PDF isn't protected, so you can add a password before saving.
    • Pages you split or extract from a protected PDF keep its password.
    • PDFs that restrict changes but open without a password now open for reading, including ones protected with 256-bit AES that couldn't be opened before.
    • Protected PDFs are checked before they replace anything: the new file must open with its password and not without it. If saving fails, the original stays as it was.
    • PDFs protected with the older RC4 method keep their passwords and permissions and are upgraded to 256-bit AES when you save changes.

    Security

    • Passwords are used only on your PC, for that file. They are never saved, never written to logs, never kept in the undo history and never sent anywhere.
    • The unlocked content of a protected PDF is kept in memory only; the working copy on disk stays encrypted.
    • Baracut Reader never guesses, recovers or gets around a password. PDFs protected with certificates, and damaged security information, are explained rather than opened.

    Known issues

    • A forgotten password can't be recovered, and PDFs protected with certificates can't be opened.
    • If you open a PDF with its permissions password and it has a different password to open, you're asked for that password when you save, so it can be kept.
    • PDFs that Baracut Reader protects always allow copying text for accessibility tools such as screen readers, as the PDF standard asks.

  2. Version 1.5.0

    27 September 2026

    Combine PDFs, rearrange their pages, split documents and extract exactly the pages you need, all on your PC.

    New

    • Combine PDFs: choose Organize, then Combine PDFs, or drag several PDFs from File Explorer onto the window. Pick as many files as you like at once, and add more at any time.
    • See every page before anything is combined: each file gets a letter, and its pages show as thumbnails (A1, A2, B1 …) with a running total, such as "4 PDFs · 126 pages".
    • Put files in order by dragging them, with the arrow buttons, or with Alt+Up and Alt+Down. Move single pages anywhere, even between files, and leave out pages you don't need. Every change can be undone, and your files stay exactly as they are.
    • The combined PDF opens as a new document, so you can check it before saving it where you want.
    • Split a PDF into parts of a few pages each, by page ranges such as "1-10, 11-20", or by the pages you selected. You see the file names before anything is saved.
    • Extract pages such as "20-30" or "1-5, 8, 12-14" into a new PDF, or save each page as its own PDF. You can also remove them from the document afterwards, which you can undo.
    • Insert pages from another PDF by choosing all of them, typing a list, or clicking the pages you want, or drop a PDF between the page thumbnails to put its pages right there.
    • Password-protected PDFs can be combined, and their pages inserted, once you enter the password. A PDF whose author doesn't allow copying needs its owner password.

    Improved

    • Organize in the top bar gathers Combine, Insert pages, Extract and Split in one place.
    • When files you're saving already exist, you're asked once for all of them: keep both, replace or skip. Nothing is replaced without asking.
    • Splitting and saving several PDFs shows real progress and can be cancelled. If something goes wrong or you cancel, no half-written files are left behind.
    • Pages are copied as they are: text stays text, drawings stay sharp, and page sizes and orientations don't change. Bookmarks, links between pages and form fields come along.
    • Large combinations with hundreds of pages stay responsive: page previews are made only for the pages you see.

    Fixed

    • A failed extraction could stop the window from closing afterwards. It now closes normally.

    Security

    • Combining, splitting and extracting happen on your PC. Your PDFs are never uploaded, and the files you start from are only read, never changed.
    • Passwords are used only to open the file on your PC and are never saved. The app never tries to guess or get around a password.

    Known issues

    • A combined PDF is a new document, so digital signatures in the files you combine aren't carried over; their fields stay, unsigned. Page labels and layer settings aren't carried over either.
    • Password-protected PDFs can't be opened for editing yet, and new PDFs aren't password-protected. Password tools will come in a later version.
    • In PDFs saved by splitting, or as one PDF per page, your added text, drawings and images become part of the pages: other apps show them as usual, but they can't be edited separately afterwards.

  3. Version 1.4.0

    26 September 2026

    A new, cleaner window while updates install, with the Baracut Reader logo and a live progress bar.

    Improved

    • Installing an update now shows a clean, modern window instead of the old setup box: the Baracut Reader logo, the version being installed, and a slim progress bar with the percentage. It follows your light or dark Windows setting, and Baracut Reader opens again as soon as it's done.
    • An update can no longer be cancelled halfway, for example by an accidental key press, which could leave the app partly updated.

  4. Version 1.3.0

    26 September 2026

    Sign PDFs with your handwritten, typed or uploaded signature and your initials, saved on your PC for next time.

    New

    • Sign documents: choose Sign in the tool bar on the left (or press S) to open the Sign panel with your saved signatures and initials.
    • Create a signature by drawing it with a mouse, pen or finger, by typing your name in one of a few handwriting styles, or by uploading a PNG or JPEG picture of it. Initials work the same way.
    • Check your signature before anything is saved: you see exactly what goes on the page, trimmed and with a transparent background. Name it, and save it for next time or use it just this once.
    • Pictures of a signature on white paper can have their background made transparent, and the preview shows the result first.
    • Place a signature by clicking where it goes. A faint preview follows the pointer so you can line it up with the signature line. Keep clicking to place it on more pages, which is handy for initialing every page, then press Esc or choose Done.
    • Move a signature, resize it without stretching it, duplicate it, or replace it with another saved signature in the same place. Add date puts today's date next to it.
    • Remove a signature from a page without touching your saved signature, and delete saved signatures from the Sign panel whenever you like. Every change to the document can be undone.
    • Signatures are saved in the PDF: other PDF apps show and print them where you placed them, with a transparent background, and they stay editable when you reopen the file in Baracut Reader.

    Improved

    • Everything you add, signatures included, keeps its place when you zoom, on any display scaling, and stays with its page when pages are moved or turned.
    • The keyboard shortcuts reference (F1) lists the signing shortcuts.

    Security

    • Saved signatures stay on your PC, in your user folder, encrypted with Windows data protection for your Windows account, so other accounts on the PC can't read them. They are never uploaded, and uninstalling the app deletes them.

    Known issues

    • These are electronic signatures: a picture of your signature on the page. They don't use certificates and don't prove who signed or that the document hasn't changed since. Certificate-based digital signatures will come in a later version.
    • Baracut Reader 1.2.0 and earlier show signatures as ordinary images.

  5. Version 1.2.0

    26 September 2026

    Add photos, logos and screenshots to your PDFs, then move, resize, rotate and crop them.

    New

    • Insert images into PDF pages: choose Image in the tool bar on the left, drop an image file onto a page, or paste one with Ctrl+V. PNG and JPEG images work, as well as BMP, GIF, TIFF and WebP.
    • A new image keeps its proportions, is scaled down to fit the page and appears where you're looking. Photos taken sideways on a phone come in the right way up.
    • Move images by dragging them or with the arrow keys, and resize them by their corners or by typing a width and height. They keep their proportions unless you hold Shift or unlock them.
    • Rotate images left or right, and crop them: choose Crop or double-click an image, drag the crop marks, then press Enter. Reset crop brings back the whole picture.
    • Replace an image with another file while keeping its place, duplicate it, change its opacity, or delete it. Every change can be undone.
    • Images are saved in the PDF: other PDF apps show and print them as you see them. Photos keep their original quality, transparent PNGs stay transparent, and images stay editable when you reopen the file in Baracut Reader.
    • Copy what you added with Ctrl+C and paste it on any page with Ctrl+V.

    Improved

    • Images, like everything you add, stay with their page when pages are moved, turned, copied or deleted.
    • The keyboard shortcuts reference (F1) lists the new image shortcuts.

    Known issues

    • Earlier versions of Baracut Reader can't open files saved with images. Update to this version to open and edit them.
    • Images turn in quarter turns only.
    • A crop hides part of a picture but keeps it in the file, so the crop can be changed later. Don't use cropping to remove sensitive content.

  6. Version 1.1.0

    26 September 2026

    Draw on your PDFs with a pen, lines, arrows, rectangles and ellipses, in the colors and widths you choose.

    New

    • Draw freehand with the pen, using a mouse, a pen or touch. Strokes are smoothed and stay sharp at any zoom.
    • Draw straight lines and arrows. Hold Shift to keep them at 45° angles.
    • Draw rectangles and ellipses, as outlines or filled. Hold Shift for a square or a circle.
    • Choose the color, line width and opacity of each drawing, and a fill color and opacity for shapes, in the bar above the page.
    • Move drawings, resize them with their handles or by the ends of a line, duplicate or delete them, and bring them forward or send them backward. Every change can be undone.
    • Drawings are saved in the PDF: other PDF apps show and print them exactly as you see them, and they stay editable when you reopen the file in Baracut Reader.
    • New keyboard shortcuts: P, L, A, R and O for the pen, line, arrow, rectangle and ellipse, and Ctrl+D to duplicate what's selected.

    Improved

    • Text and highlights you added can be duplicated too, from the bar above the page, the right-click menu or Ctrl+D.
    • The right-click menu for anything you added now also offers Duplicate, Bring forward and Send backward.
    • Esc now first cancels a drag in progress, then clears the selection, then returns to the Select tool.

    Known issues

    • Earlier versions of Baracut Reader can't open files saved with drawings. Update to this version to open and edit them.

  7. Version 1.0.0

    26 September 2026

    The first release of Baracut Reader, a focused PDF editor for Windows.

    New

    • Open PDFs from the app, by dropping a file on the window, or with Open with in File Explorer.
    • Replace existing text in supported PDFs, and add your own text in Arial, Times New Roman or Courier New.
    • Highlight areas of a page in a choice of colors, with adjustable opacity.
    • Select, move, restyle and delete the text and highlights you add.
    • Organize pages: reorder by dragging, rotate, duplicate, delete, insert blank pages or pages from another PDF, and extract pages to a new PDF.
    • Undo and redo every change.
    • Save a standard PDF that other PDF readers can open. Text and highlights you add stay editable when you reopen the file in Baracut Reader.
    • Light and dark themes that follow Windows, and support for Windows high contrast themes.
    • Keyboard shortcuts for the main actions. Press F1 to see them all.
    • Built-in updates: Baracut Reader checks for new versions and installs them for you, after your documents are saved.

A PDF editor for Windows: edit text, highlight, organize pages and save standard PDFs.
