Changelog

What changed in each version

Installed copies update themselves, so the newest version is usually a click away. Download 1.2.0

  1. Version 1.2.0

    Current

    26 September 2026

    Add photos, logos and screenshots to your PDFs, then move, resize, rotate and crop them.

    New

    • Insert images into PDF pages: choose Image in the tool bar on the left, drop an image file onto a page, or paste one with Ctrl+V. PNG and JPEG images work, as well as BMP, GIF, TIFF and WebP.
    • A new image keeps its proportions, is scaled down to fit the page and appears where you're looking. Photos taken sideways on a phone come in the right way up.
    • Move images by dragging them or with the arrow keys, and resize them by their corners or by typing a width and height. They keep their proportions unless you hold Shift or unlock them.
    • Rotate images left or right, and crop them: choose Crop or double-click an image, drag the crop marks, then press Enter. Reset crop brings back the whole picture.
    • Replace an image with another file while keeping its place, duplicate it, change its opacity, or delete it. Every change can be undone.
    • Images are saved in the PDF: other PDF apps show and print them as you see them. Photos keep their original quality, transparent PNGs stay transparent, and images stay editable when you reopen the file in Baracut Reader.
    • Copy what you added with Ctrl+C and paste it on any page with Ctrl+V.

    Improved

    • Images, like everything you add, stay with their page when pages are moved, turned, copied or deleted.
    • The keyboard shortcuts reference (F1) lists the new image shortcuts.

    Known issues

    • Earlier versions of Baracut Reader can't open files saved with images. Update to this version to open and edit them.
    • Images turn in quarter turns only.
    • A crop hides part of a picture but keeps it in the file, so the crop can be changed later. Don't use cropping to remove sensitive content.

  2. Version 1.1.0

    26 September 2026

    Draw on your PDFs with a pen, lines, arrows, rectangles and ellipses, in the colors and widths you choose.

    New

    • Draw freehand with the pen, using a mouse, a pen or touch. Strokes are smoothed and stay sharp at any zoom.
    • Draw straight lines and arrows. Hold Shift to keep them at 45° angles.
    • Draw rectangles and ellipses, as outlines or filled. Hold Shift for a square or a circle.
    • Choose the color, line width and opacity of each drawing, and a fill color and opacity for shapes, in the bar above the page.
    • Move drawings, resize them with their handles or by the ends of a line, duplicate or delete them, and bring them forward or send them backward. Every change can be undone.
    • Drawings are saved in the PDF: other PDF apps show and print them exactly as you see them, and they stay editable when you reopen the file in Baracut Reader.
    • New keyboard shortcuts: P, L, A, R and O for the pen, line, arrow, rectangle and ellipse, and Ctrl+D to duplicate what's selected.

    Improved

    • Text and highlights you added can be duplicated too, from the bar above the page, the right-click menu or Ctrl+D.
    • The right-click menu for anything you added now also offers Duplicate, Bring forward and Send backward.
    • Esc now first cancels a drag in progress, then clears the selection, then returns to the Select tool.

    Known issues

    • Earlier versions of Baracut Reader can't open files saved with drawings. Update to this version to open and edit them.

  3. Version 1.0.0

    26 September 2026

    The first release of Baracut Reader, a focused PDF editor for Windows.

    New

    • Open PDFs from the app, by dropping a file on the window, or with Open with in File Explorer.
    • Replace existing text in supported PDFs, and add your own text in Arial, Times New Roman or Courier New.
    • Highlight areas of a page in a choice of colors, with adjustable opacity.
    • Select, move, restyle and delete the text and highlights you add.
    • Organize pages: reorder by dragging, rotate, duplicate, delete, insert blank pages or pages from another PDF, and extract pages to a new PDF.
    • Undo and redo every change.
    • Save a standard PDF that other PDF readers can open. Text and highlights you add stay editable when you reopen the file in Baracut Reader.
    • Light and dark themes that follow Windows, and support for Windows high contrast themes.
    • Keyboard shortcuts for the main actions. Press F1 to see them all.
    • Built-in updates: Baracut Reader checks for new versions and installs them for you, after your documents are saved.

A PDF editor for Windows: edit text, highlight, organize pages and save standard PDFs.
