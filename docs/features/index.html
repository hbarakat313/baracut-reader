Features

Everything in Baracut Reader 1.8.3

A focused set of tools for the PDFs you already have. This page lists what the current version does, what it doesn't do yet, and what is planned.

Editing

Edit existing text
Choose Edit text, click an outlined line and type the replacement. It keeps the line's position, in Arial, Times New Roman or Courier New.
Add text
Click anywhere on a page to type. Pick the font, size and color; Shift+Enter adds a line.
Highlight
Drag across an area to highlight it, in a choice of colors with adjustable opacity.
Select and adjust
Move what you added with the mouse or the arrow keys, change its style in the bar above the page, or delete it.
Undo and redo
Every change, including page changes, is one step you can undo.

Drawing and shapes

Pen
Draw freehand with a mouse, pen or touch. Strokes are smoothed and stay sharp at any zoom.
Lines and arrows
Drag to draw a straight line or an arrow. Hold Shift for 45° angles.
Rectangles and ellipses
Drag to draw a shape, as an outline or filled. Hold Shift for a square or a circle.
Color, width and opacity
Set them before you draw or change them afterwards, in the bar above the page. A shape's fill has its own color and opacity.
Move, resize and duplicate
Drag a drawing, pull its handles or the ends of a line, copy it with Ctrl+D, or bring it forward. Every change can be undone.
Part of the saved PDF
Drawings appear in any PDF reader and print as shown, and stay editable when you reopen the file in Baracut Reader.

Images

Insert images
Add a photo, logo or screenshot from a file, by dropping it on a page, or with Ctrl+V. PNG and JPEG, as well as BMP, GIF, TIFF and WebP.
Sized to fit, upright
A new image keeps its proportions and is scaled down to fit the page. Phone photos come in the right way up.
Move and resize
Drag an image anywhere, pull its corners, or type its width and height. Its proportions stay unless you choose to stretch it.
Rotate and crop
Turn an image a quarter turn at a time and crop it to what matters. A crop can be changed or reset later.
Replace, duplicate, opacity
Swap in another picture without losing its place, copy it, or make it see-through. Every change can be undone.
Part of the saved PDF
Images appear in any PDF reader and print as shown. Photos keep their original quality, and transparent PNGs stay transparent.

Signatures

Draw, type or upload
Sign with a mouse, pen or finger, type your name in a handwriting style, or use a picture of your signature. Initials too.
Saved for next time
Keep your signature and initials on your PC, encrypted for your Windows account, and use them on any PDF. Nothing is uploaded.
Place, then keep going
Choose a signature and click where it goes. Keep clicking to initial every page, and add today's date beside it.
Move, resize, replace
Drag it onto the line, resize it without stretching it, or swap it for another saved signature in the same place. Every change can be undone.
Part of the saved PDF
Signatures appear in any PDF reader and print as shown, with a transparent background, and stay editable when you reopen the file in Baracut Reader.
Electronic, not certificate-based
These are visual electronic signatures, a picture of your signature on the page. They don't use certificates and don't prove who signed.

Pages

Reorder
Drag thumbnails, or move pages up and down from the keyboard.
Rotate, duplicate, delete
Act on one page or a selection. Rotation turns the page in the PDF, not just on screen.
Insert
Add a blank A4 or Letter page, or pages from another PDF: all of them, a list such as “1-3, 5”, or the ones you click. Drop a PDF between the thumbnails to put its pages right there.

Combine, split and extract

Combine PDFs
Pick several PDFs at once, or drag them in from File Explorer, and see every page before anything is combined. The result opens as a new document for you to check and save.
Your order, down to the page
Drag files into order, and move single pages anywhere, even between files. Leave out pages you don't need. Every change can be undone, and your files stay as they are.
Split
Divide a PDF into parts of a few pages each, by page ranges, or by the pages you selected. You see the file names before anything is saved.
Extract pages
Save pages such as “20-30” or “1-5, 8, 12-14” as a new PDF, or each page as its own PDF. Optionally remove them from the document afterwards, which you can undo.
Nothing overwritten by accident
When files already exist you choose once: keep both, replace or skip. If something goes wrong or you cancel, no half-written files are left behind.
Quality kept
Pages are copied as they are: text stays text, drawings stay sharp, and page sizes and orientations don't change. Bookmarks, links and form fields come along.
Protected PDFs
A password-protected PDF can be combined or have its pages inserted once you enter its password. The password isn't stored, and it is never guessed or bypassed.
On your PC
Combining, splitting and extracting happen on your computer. Your PDFs are never uploaded.

Password protection

Open protected PDFs
Open and edit password-protected PDFs when you have the password. A wrong password is simply asked for again; nothing is opened half-way.
Protect a PDF
Require a password to open a PDF before you share it. It's encrypted with 256-bit AES, the method of the current PDF standard, which current PDF readers and web browsers open.
Change or remove protection
Change the password, or remove protection, when you opened the PDF with its password. Removing it is always your choice, and so is saving an unprotected copy.
Protection kept when you save
Edit a protected PDF and save it: it keeps its password and restrictions. Pages you split or extract from it are protected the same way.
Restrict printing, copying and changes
An optional permissions password asks PDF readers to restrict printing, copying and changes. Readers are expected to honor it, but not every app does.
Passwords stay yours
Passwords are used only on your PC, for that file. They are never saved, never written to logs and never sent anywhere, and the app never guesses or gets around one.

Forms and Fill & Sign

Fill in PDF forms
Type in text fields, check boxes, choose options and pick from lists. Fields are found when you open the PDF, and each answer can be undone.
Saved in the form
Your answers are stored in the PDF's own form, so other PDF apps show them and the form can still be filled later.
Tab from field to field
Move through a form with Tab and Shift+Tab. The form bar shows how many required fields are left, and Review takes you to the next one.
Fill & Sign any form
On scanned or flat forms, add text, checkmarks, X marks and the date wherever you click, then move or resize them.
Dates, initials and signatures
Add today's date in your region's format, and your saved initials or signature. In a form's signature field, your signature is fitted into it.
Flattened copy
Save a copy where the answers become part of the pages, for sending or archiving. The form you have open keeps its fields.

Search and navigation

Find in the whole document
Click Find, or press Ctrl+F, and type. Every page is searched as you type, and the page jumps to the nearest match from where you are.
See every match
Matches are highlighted on the pages, with the current one outlined. A count shows where you are, such as “3 of 27”.
A list of results
Open the results list to see each match with the words around it, grouped by page. Click one to go there.
Finds what you mean
Capital letters and accents don't have to match, phrases are found across line breaks, and a word split with a hyphen at the end of a line is still found. Match case and whole words are one click away.
Your own text and form answers too
Text you added and answers typed into form fields are found as soon as they are there, without saving first.
Go to a page, and back again
Ctrl+G jumps to a page number. Back and Forward (Alt+Left and Alt+Right) return to where you were before a jump or a search.
On your PC
Searching happens on your computer, in memory. Nothing you search for or find is saved or sent anywhere.

Saving

A standard PDF
Saved files open in any PDF reader, which shows the finished pages.
Edits stay editable
Reopen a file in Baracut Reader and your added text, highlights, drawings, images, signatures, checkmarks and X marks are still individually editable.
Careful saving
A save is written to a temporary file first and only replaces the original once it succeeded. If it fails, nothing is lost.
Unsaved changes
Closing or opening another file asks Save, Don't save or Cancel, only when there is something to save.

Viewing and comfort

Zoom and fit
From 25% to 300%, with Fit width and Fit page. Zooming keeps your place.
Thumbnails and navigation
A page panel, page numbers, Go to page and keyboard navigation for long documents, with Back and Forward between the places you jumped to.
Open your way
Use Open, drop a file on the window, or choose Open with in File Explorer.
Light, dark and high contrast
Follows your Windows theme, including high contrast themes.
Keyboard shortcuts
For the main actions. Press F1 in the app to see them all.

Installation and updates

Installs without admin rights
For your Windows account, with a Start menu entry and an optional desktop shortcut.
Built-in updates
A quiet notice when a new version is out; background download; verification before install; your work saved first.
Clean uninstall
From Settings, like any Windows app. Your documents are never touched.

Current limits

Worth knowing before you rely on it.

  • Scanned pages can be highlighted and annotated, but their text can't be changed: that needs text recognition (OCR).
  • Edited text doesn't reflow paragraphs, and the original font is replaced by Arial, Times New Roman or Courier New.
  • Some text can't be edited, such as rotated or vertical lines and text drawn as outlines. The app tells you when a line isn't editable.
  • Text you replace or delete is kept inside the file so edits stay editable; this is not a redaction tool.
  • Drawings become part of the page. Other PDF apps show and print them exactly, but can't edit them; reopen the file in Baracut Reader to change them.
  • Images turn in quarter turns. A crop hides part of a picture but keeps it in the file, so the crop can be changed later; it isn't a way to remove sensitive content.
  • Signatures are electronic: a picture of your signature on the page. Certificate-based digital signatures, which prove who signed and that nothing changed afterwards, aren't supported yet.
  • Baracut Reader can't recover a forgotten password or open a PDF without its password, and it can't open PDFs protected with certificates.
  • A PDF with a separate permissions password opens for reading with its password to open; changing it, or its protection, needs the permissions password.
  • Forms are filled without running their scripts: calculated fields aren't updated and entries aren't checked by the form's own rules. Forms made with XFA can't be filled, and new form fields can't be created.
  • Saving changes to a PDF with a certificate-based digital signature makes that signature invalid.
  • Search finds the text a PDF stores. Scanned pages and text inside pictures have none, so they aren't found; the app says when pages have no text to search.
  • A combined PDF is a new document, so digital signatures in the files you combine aren't carried over. Page labels and layer settings aren't carried over either.
