What it does

The edits people actually need

A few tools on a quiet rail, a document that stays in focus, and undo for every change.

Edit existing text

Click a line in the PDF and type. The new words replace the original in the same spot.

Add text, images and signatures

Type anywhere, place a photo or logo, or sign with your saved signature, then move, resize or remove it.

Highlight and draw

Mark what matters with highlights, a pen, lines, arrows and shapes, in the colors you choose.

Combine and split

Combine multiple PDFs, rearrange pages, split documents, and extract exactly the pages you need.

How it works

Open, edit, save. Nothing in between.

Change the words on the page

Choose Edit text and the lines you can change are outlined. Click one, type, and press Enter. The replacement uses Arial, Times New Roman or Courier New. Paragraphs don't reflow, so short changes work best.

Mark it up, and change your mind later

Add text anywhere and drag across an area to highlight it. Everything you add can be selected, moved, restyled or removed, and it stays editable the next time you open the file in Baracut Reader. Other PDF readers show the finished pages.

Put pages in order

Drag thumbnails to reorder pages. Rotate, duplicate or delete them, insert blank pages or pages from another PDF, and extract pages into a new file. Every change is one step you can undo.

Sign where it says sign

Draw your signature, type it or use a picture of it, once. Then choose Sign, click where it goes and keep clicking to initial every page. It's saved on your PC, encrypted for your Windows account, and never uploaded. These are electronic signatures, not certificate-based digital signatures.

Your files

Your documents stay on your computer

Baracut Reader is a desktop app. It opens and saves files where you choose, and it has nothing to upload them to.

How privacy works

  • Local by design

    No account, no cloud storage. Opening, editing and saving all happen on your PC, offline too.

  • No tracking

    The app has no analytics and sends no usage data. The website sets no cookies.

  • One connection, for updates

    About once a day it asks whether a newer version exists. You can turn that off.

Updates

New features arrive without reinstalling

When a new version is out, Baracut Reader tells you quietly and lets you decide when.

  • A small notice, never a pop-up in the middle of your work.
  • Downloads in the background while you keep editing.
  • Checked against signed release information before anything is installed.
  • Asks you to save unsaved changes, then updates and reopens.

Latest release

Version 1.8.3

Released 3 October 2026

Edit text works on many more PDFs and keeps tables and forms in place, and Find finds words typed with plain quotes and apostrophes.

  • Edit text no longer turns down a whole page because of one thing on it. Pages with grouped content, layers or tagged text, PDF 2.0 files, lines whose spaces are stored as tabs, and text made bold with an outline can now be edited. Text that still can't be changed is left alone, and clicking it tells you why.
  • Words that a PDF places apart without a space character (common in PDFs made with LaTeX and some converters) now show their spaces when you edit the line. Before, the words ran together, and were saved that way.
  • A table row or a form's labels drawn in one piece are now edited cell by cell. Before, changing one number could pull the rest of the row over to it.
  • Lines are no longer cut into pieces where the space between two words comes from another font, as in many Word documents.

Questions

Good to know

Which versions of Windows are supported?

Windows 10 (version 22H2) or Windows 11, on 64-bit Intel or AMD (x64) PCs. Other processor types aren't offered yet.

Do I need an internet connection?

Only to download the installer and updates. Opening, editing and saving PDFs works entirely offline. If the app can't reach the update service, it says so and keeps working normally.

How do updates work?

About once a day, Baracut Reader checks whether a newer version exists. If there is one, a small notice appears; nothing downloads or installs until you choose to. The download runs in the background while you keep working, and it is verified against signed release information before it can be installed. When you install, you're asked to save any unsaved changes, then the app closes, updates and opens again.

You can check at any time from More options → Check for updates, and turn automatic checks off in the Updates window.

Does Baracut Reader upload my PDFs anywhere?

No. Your documents are opened, edited and saved on your computer. There is no account, no cloud storage and no analytics. The only internet connections the app makes are the update check and update downloads. Like any web request they reach the server with your computer's IP address, and they include the app's version number, but nothing about your documents. The privacy page has the details.
